The Complete Process

How CollabX Turns Problems into Impact

Follow the journey of a civic problem — from the moment a citizen reports it, to the day a solution reaches their community.

1
Citizen Report

A Citizen Reports a Real Problem

Any citizen can report a civic issue in their community — a pothole, water shortage, sanitation problem, broken streetlight, or any public challenge. Reports can be submitted in multiple formats.

📝 Text description 🎤 Voice notes 📷 Photos & videos 📍 GPS location 📎 Supporting documents 📴 Works offline too
2
AI Processing

AI Validates, Categorizes, and Prioritizes

The moment a report is submitted, our AI engine analyzes it — filtering out fake reports, categorizing by domain, scoring urgency, and detecting duplicates. Every report gets a priority score based on real-world urgency.

✓ Fake-report filtering 🏷️ Auto-categorization ⚠️ Priority scoring 🔍 Duplicate detection 📊 Real-time processing
3
Smart Matching

Challenges Reach the Right University

Our platform matches each challenge to universities with the right expertise — engineering, environmental science, public health, urban planning, or any relevant domain. The right problem reaches the right team, fast.

🎓 Expertise-based matching 📍 Geographic proximity 🏛️ Government publishing 📢 Public challenge board
4
University Solutions

Student Teams Propose Solutions

Faculty and student teams from matched universities study the challenge and propose detailed solutions — with technical approach, budget estimate, timeline, and implementation plan. Each proposal is research-backed.

👥 Student teams + faculty mentors 📋 Detailed research-backed proposals 💰 Budget & timeline estimate 🤖 AI screening of each proposal
5
Expert Selection

Domain Experts Choose the Best Solution

A panel of domain experts reviews the AI-screened solutions, verifies the scores, and selects the best proposal. This AI + Expert combination ensures both scale and accountability in every selection.

⭐ Verified expert panel 📊 AI scores + expert review 🏆 Best solution selected 🔓 Transparent selection process
6
Industry + Impact

Industry Funds, Pilots Run, Citizens Benefit

Industry partners provide CSR funding, testing equipment, and pilot infrastructure. The selected solution moves through development → prototype → pilot → scale. Citizens give feedback, milestones are tracked, and successful solutions are deployed across regions.

🏭 Industry CSR funding 🔬 Testing & prototyping 🏛️ Government pilot approval 📈 Large-scale deployment 💬 Citizen feedback loop
